# Outcome

A workflow adapter can evaluate an explicit QA applicability decision and receive immutable structured evidence for either a justified not-applicable outcome or every attempted required QA scenario.

# Why now

This delivers AC-007 from `docs/PRODUCT.md` after bounded fix-loop policy and establishes the explicit QA gate needed before PR-review and merge gates are added.

# Scope

## In

- Add a reusable QA-phase runner with an explicit required or not-applicable decision.
- Require a substantive reason for not-applicable decisions and reject scenario execution in that mode.
- Run a bounded, ordered set of named required scenarios through an adapter callback.
- Return immutable structured evidence for every attempted scenario, including outcome, reason, timing, and optional bounded artifact references.
- Stop on failed or blocked results and fail closed on malformed results or callback failures.
- Cover decisions, successful scenarios, stopping behavior, limits, malformed/untrusted results, callback failures, and immutable evidence with deterministic tests.

## Out

- Browser tooling, screenshots, provider-specific QA adapters, durable artifact persistence, resume, automatic fixes, or integration with the AC-006 fix loop.
- PR review, GitHub, merge/deployment gates, or multi-task orchestration.
- Treating deterministic verification as QA or inferring applicability from model prose.

# Implementation constraints

- Applicability is explicit policy input, never inferred by the runner.
- Required QA must contain at least one uniquely named scenario and may contain at most 32.
- Scenario callbacks receive bounded, validated scenario definitions and cannot add evidence outside the declared scenario.
- Reasons and artifact references are bounded, non-empty, NUL-free strings; callback errors do not leak exception details.
- A failed or blocked scenario stops later scenarios; every attempted scenario has an ordered record.
- Implement the active remediation only from `fix/AC-007-review-findings` in its isolated worktree.
